Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information AVZERA Digital Solutions collects, why we collect it, how we store it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 19 November 2026 Applies to: this website and AVZERA client engagements

1. Who we are

AVZERA Digital Solutions (“AVZERA”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is an independent digital services brand founded and operated by Vikas Vivan. We help local businesses with Google Business Profile optimization, custom websites, and WhatsApp/AI automation.

Data Fiduciary: AVZERA Digital Solutions, Avantibai Chowk, Bhilai, Chhattisgarh, India.
Privacy contact: grievance@avzera.com · +91 78983 32744

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, WhatsApp number, business name and city.
  • The service you are interested in and any message you send.
  • Consent records showing that you agreed to be contacted.
  • Project information you share during an engagement, such as business details, photos, logos, website links, Google Business Profile links and account access.
  • Billing and invoice details when you become a client. We do not store full card numbers or UPI PINs.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and timestamps.
  • Server logs used for security, debugging and abuse prevention.
  • If analytics is enabled, aggregated usage data. We aim to use privacy-friendly settings and will not sell this data.

Please do not send sensitive personal information (such as health records, government ID numbers, financial account numbers or passwords) through the website form or WhatsApp unless we specifically ask for it for a project and explain why.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a free audit or quote.
  • To deliver, support and improve the services you engage us for.
  • To send project updates, invoices and service-related messages.
  • To keep records for accounting, tax and legal compliance.
  • To protect the website and services against spam, fraud and abuse.
  • To improve our website, offers and customer experience.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your phone number for unrelated spam.

4. Legal basis for processing

Where you submit an enquiry or ask us to contact you on WhatsApp, we process your name, number, business details and message on the basis of your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.

For active client projects, we process business contact, project and billing information to perform our contract with you, to comply with tax, accounting and other legal obligations, and for other legitimate uses permitted under applicable law. We take consent separately where the law requires it.

This policy is written with reference to India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules. It is a plain-language policy, not legal advice.

Consent notice

The enquiry form on our story page asks you to tick a consent box before it opens WhatsApp. The box is unticked by default. By ticking it, you agree that AVZERA may contact you on WhatsApp about that specific enquiry. We do not add you to a marketing list, sell your number, or contact you for unrelated purposes. You can withdraw consent by replying “STOP” on WhatsApp or by emailing grievance@avzera.com.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website is designed to work with minimal cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent before non-essential cookies are set. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work as well.

6. Sharing information

We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers that help us host the website, store enquiries, send messages, process payments, or analyse usage.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants or lawyers, where necessary.
  • Government authorities or courts when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

These providers are expected to use the information only for the service they provide. Some providers may process data outside India. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to use providers with appropriate safeguards.

7. How long we keep information

  • Enquiries that do not become projects: up to 24 months, unless you ask us to delete them earlier.
  • Client and invoice records: for as long as needed to provide services and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements, typically up to 8 years.
  • Marketing consent records: until you withdraw consent or we no longer need the record, and in any case no longer than 180 days after a valid deletion request.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we hold your personal information and give you access to it.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete information that we no longer need for a lawful purpose.
  • Withdraw consent for future contact or processing.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Raise a grievance about how your information is handled.

Send requests on WhatsApp at +91 78983 32744 with the subject “Privacy Request”. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India or another competent authority.
