Refund Policy

Clear terms for the 40% advance, the 7-day review window, cancellations and third-party costs.

Last updated: 19 November 2026 Applies to: all AVZERA projects

1. The short version

  • You pay 40% at kickoff and 60% after the review window.
  • You get a 7-day review window after delivery to report genuine issues within the agreed scope.
  • If we fail to deliver the agreed work, the advance is refunded under this policy, minus non-refundable third-party costs and work already approved.
  • Domain, hosting, API credits, ad spend and other third-party costs are non-refundable.
  • Refunds are processed within 7–10 working days after approval.

2. What the 40% advance covers

The 40% advance reserves your project slot and covers discovery, planning, design and setup work. It is not a security deposit. Once work starts, part of the advance is consumed by time and third-party costs.

3. 7-day review window

After we deliver the first complete version (website staging link, GBP setup summary, or automation demo), you have 7 days to review it and report issues. We will fix genuine defects and items that are part of the agreed scope.

The review window is not a “results guarantee”. Google rankings, traffic, leads and revenue depend on factors outside our control and are not covered by this policy.

4. When a refund may apply

  • We fail to deliver the agreed deliverables and do not fix the gap within a reasonable time after you report it.
  • We cancel the project without cause before completing the agreed work.
  • You cancel before work starts and before any third-party costs are incurred.
  • A duplicate payment was made by mistake.

5. When a refund may not apply

  • You changed your mind after work started or after approving a design/staging version.
  • You expected a specific Google ranking, traffic number, lead count or revenue result.
  • You did not provide content, access or approvals, causing the delay.
  • The issue is caused by a third-party platform (Google, WhatsApp, hosting, payment gateway) or by your own changes after handover.
  • The work was custom and already delivered/approved, and the request is for a change of direction rather than a defect.
  • Domain, hosting, API credits, ad spend, stock images, fonts or other third-party costs have already been purchased.

6. Cancellation

If you cancel before work starts

You get a refund of the advance minus any payment gateway fees and third-party costs already incurred.

If you cancel after work starts

We will calculate the value of work completed and approved up to the cancellation date. You pay for that work plus non-refundable third-party costs. Any remaining balance of the advance is refunded.

If we cancel without cause

We refund the portion of the advance for work not completed, minus third-party costs already incurred.

7. How to request a refund

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to +91 78983 32744 with the subject “Refund Request”.
  2. Include your name, business name, project details, payment reference and the reason for the request.
  3. We will review the request and respond within 5 working days.
  4. If approved, the refund is processed within 7–10 working days to the original payment method.

Please do not file a chargeback before contacting us. A chargeback can freeze the project and delay a resolution for both sides.

8. Third-party costs

The following are generally non-refundable because they are paid to third parties: domain registration, hosting, SSL certificates, payment gateway fees, API usage credits, advertising spend, stock assets, software subscriptions and government fees. We will tell you before purchasing any third-party service on your behalf.

9. Chargebacks and disputes

If you believe a payment was incorrect, contact us first. We will share invoices, delivery records and communication history. If we cannot resolve the issue, either party may use the dispute process in our Terms & Conditions.
